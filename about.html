Who We Are

Kenyan food deserves a seat at the fine dining table.

Savanna Grill was born in 2019 from a simple idea: Kenyan food deserves a seat at the fine dining table.

Founded by Chef Amani Odera, a Culinary Institute of America graduate who returned to Nairobi with a mission, the restaurant reimagines classic Kenyan dishes with modern techniques and presentation without losing the soul of the food.

Our ingredients are sourced from farmers across Kenya. The tilapia comes from Lake Victoria, the vegetables are from organic farms in Limuru and the coffee is single-origin from Nyeri.

What started as a 20-seat pop-up in Westlands has grown into an 80-seat dining experience with a ground-floor dining room and open-air rooftop overlooking the Ngong Hills.
